# Business Insights

> **SYNTHETIC DATA - demo results only.** The figures below come from a fully synthetic dataset (200 customers, 476,202 transactions) generated by a public generator. They must NOT be presented as real-bank performance or used for real business decisions. See `data/raw/SOURCE.md`.

> **Currency note.** The source mixes USD, GBP and EUR. All value KPIs are expressed in a **USD-equivalent** using *illustrative fixed rates* (`data/reference/fx_rates.csv`), not market data. Native-currency totals are shown below and must not be added together.

## Executive summary

- The dataset contains **200 customers**; 200 have at least one transaction. A 100% activity rate is a property of the generated sample, not a retention benchmark.
- Transaction value excluding internal-transfer legs: **69,024,709.29 USD-eq.**, average **147.62**.
- Deposits **36,160,291.88**, withdrawals **32,340,704.81**, fees **523,712.60** -> net flow **3,295,874.47** (deposits minus withdrawals and fees).
- Internal transfers (8,616 legs, 6,653,666.56 across both legs) net to zero and are reported separately.
- Data-quality checks: all passed.

## Transaction and financial insights

- Complete months analysed: 36. Net flow was negative in **2** of them, so aggregate flow should be monitored together with month-level volatility.
- The final month (2026-10) is partial and is excluded from trend charts and growth rates.
- Fees are reported separately from withdrawals; internal transfers are excluded from total transaction value because each transfer appears as two offsetting legs.
- Transaction type mapping was normalized during adaptation; the original type is preserved in `source_transaction_type`.

## Anomaly observations

The screening flagged **9,148 of 476,202 rows (1.92%)**. Rules: robust z-score of log(amount) within each customer x category (recurring subscriptions, salary and transfers excluded) plus customer-day frequency far above the customer's own typical day. This is a *screening population*, not a fraud rate; review samples in context before any operational decision.

## Method and limitations

- **Synthetic data**: no behavioural conclusion transfers to a real bank.
- **FX**: fixed illustrative rates; with real data use dated market rates per transaction.
- `transaction_status` was set to `Completed` because the source has no status field (an assumption, not a source fact).
- `branches.csv` and `products.csv` are derived compatibility tables, not source master data.
- RFM thresholds are quintile-based for this demo and should be recalculated on real data.
- Statistical anomaly flags do not prove fraud.
